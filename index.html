Anomaly detection for financial and product KPIs

iGaming operator, 2026

24hinstead of several days

Deviations in deposits, withdrawals, GGR and product metrics were noticed days late, usually by someone who happened to open a dashboard. There was no single place where "normal" was defined.

Each metric is now defined once in SQL models in Snowflake, so the alert and the report use the same number. A scheduled Python job compares every daily value with a robust baseline built from the previous weeks, writes deviations to an alerts table and notifies the metric owner. Tableau shows the open alerts next to the KPI trend.

The same models feed the regular dashboards, so there is one definition of every KPI in the company.

The players who only came for the bonus

Online betting and casino operator, 2025

1 ruleCRM and fraud teams could act on

Part of the bonus budget was going to players who deposited the minimum, wagered exactly to the requirement, withdrew and never came back. Nobody could say how large that group was.

A behavioural definition in SQL, built from deposit size, wagering pattern against the requirement, withdrawal timing and activity afterwards, made the segment countable and its cost visible. Changes to the bonus terms and crediting rules followed.

The segment shrank significantly, and the bonus budget went to players who actually play. The same method is reproduced on synthetic data in the open case below.

Also: an acquisition channel review that compared channels by player value instead of cost per registration, behind a 25% lower CPA after budget reallocation; a registration-to-first-deposit funnel analysis behind a 22% conversion lift; A/B tests for bonuses, CRM campaigns and product changes.

iGaming analytics case

Synthetic data, PostgreSQL and Python, 2026

6xthe cost of a depositor from paid social against referral

A full analytics workflow on a synthetic casino dataset: 12,000 registrations, deposits, play activity, bonuses and marketing spend, with the usual problems planted in the data. Cleaning layer, cohort retention, channel economics and an A/B test of a bigger welcome bonus in SQL, plus a Python monitor that flags anomalies in daily KPIs.

Paid social buys the cheapest registrations and the most expensive depositors. The bigger welcome bonus lifts first deposits by 23% but triples the share of bonus chasers and cuts net revenue per registration from 13.9 to 4.5 EUR.
